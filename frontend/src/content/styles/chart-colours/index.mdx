In some charts, colours help differentiate between categories of data, such as
in line charts or stacked bar charts. Some visualisations use colour to
represent numerical values, such as heatmaps.

The chart palette draws from the [web palette](/styles/colour#web-palette). This
page only covers the parts specific to charts: the categorical picks, the rule
on sequential and divergent scales, and the accent colours.

## Sequential and divergent scale palette

Avoid using colour alone to visualise insights. Use a maximum of two scales in a
single chart.

The scales use the tints and shades from the [web palette](/styles/colour#web-palette).

## Using colour in charts

When choosing colours for your data visualisation:

- ensure sufficient contrast with the background and overlapping text
- avoid using colour as the only visual means of conveying information
- focus on applying colour that enhances the clarity of the data
- limit colours to avoid confusion
