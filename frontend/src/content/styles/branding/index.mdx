## Product brands in i.AI

Products in i.AI will be branded as one of two options:

- **A government department product**
- **A GOV.UK product**

The guidance here is on how to choose which brand to apply to your product, what constitues a "brand", and how to switch between them.

## Context

The context and reasoning of decisions behind these branding guidelines are recorded in the [Decisions record](/decisions-record).

Most relevant here are the following:

- [We will build off of GOV.UK Frontend](#)
- [Branding is distinct from functionality](#)
- [Branding is understood as limited to header, typography and brand colours](#)

The implication of these decisions is that it is easy to switch between the brands as the user base of the product evolves or changes.

## Government department brand

This is the default brand choice of our i.AI products. If you are in an experimental 
stage of development or unsure of which brand to choose you should go with this one 
and switch later to the GOV.UK branding

### What is my department?

If you are working with a specific government department then that that is the department 
branding you should use. E.g. your product should be called "DWP Minute" and have the DWP Header.

If you are not working with a specific department, then your department is "i.AI". You use the 
[generic header component](/components/header).


### Implementation

The Design Kit has been written with this default in mind, therefore you can read 
the [getting started guides](/get-started) to implement this brand to your product.

Overall this involves:

- Use the [generic header component](/components/header)
- Use the Helvetica Nue font
- Change the brand colour to "#1a65a6"

## GOV.UK brand

This should be adopted if:

- it's for use by the general public 
- it's for use across the public sector

This will likely happen in the later stages of a products development as they get rolled out 
to wider government departments or to the public. It is assumed therefore that most products will 
have to be moved to the GOV.UK brand rather than start there.

The Design Kit has been written with the departmental branding default in mind to avoid confusion. 
The rest of the Design Kit is still relevant to a product branded as GOV.UK apart from in the ways 
set out below which constitute moving to the GOV.UK brand

### Moving to the GOV.UK brand

We plan to add more detail to these instructions soon. For now, we have a list of things that you 
have to do to brand as a GOV.UK product.

- Name your product "GOV.UK Product Name"
- Use the [GOV.UK header component](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/header/). This has 
not been included in this site to avoid confusion but it is available with the i.AI styles
- Revert the brand colour to the GOV.UK blue
- Use GDS transport font
