## How to use this design system

This is intended to speed up your work, and avoid duplicated effort. It is a
collection of resources to help reduce solving for the same problems repeatedly
across our team.

Therefore if something in here doesn't fit your needs, or is missing, then
please design for your problem and we'll look into a way that we can add it in
here.
