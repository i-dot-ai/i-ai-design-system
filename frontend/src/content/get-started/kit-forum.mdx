---
title: "Add to the Design kit"
description: How the kit forum decides what goes into the i.AI kit.
---

The kit forum is the fortnightly group that decides what goes into the
i.AI kit. It's open to anyone at i.AI. It is not a gatekeeping committee.

Take something to the kit forum when you have:

- built a component the kit doesn't have
- adapted a kit component so that it no longer matches what the kit ships
- departed from an established GOV.UK pattern
- needed something the kit couldn't give you, even if you worked around it

Recording a departure you worked around matters as much as recording one
you built. A gap is evidence about where the kit falls short.

## Use the mechanism rather than writing it by hand

There is a mechanism, currently a Claude skill, that writes the record for
you and submits it to the kit forum. [provisional — not yet built]

Point it at what you built and it will:

- draft the decision record entry
- draft the deviation record against the 4 fixed fields
- apply a provenance tag
- raise the issue against the kit repo using the template

Check what it produces before you submit it. You're still the author.

Use it because recording happens at the point of deviation, when you're
mid-build and least inclined to write anything down. That's the entire
discipline cost of this, and the mechanism exists to remove it.

## What the kit forum does with it

- **Triage** sorts your submission into: already exists, a variant of
  something that exists, genuinely new, or out of scope. The default
  answer is to use what exists, and the burden of proof sits with the new
  thing.
- **Decide** happens at the fortnightly session. The group accepts,
  rejects, or defers with a named reason. Anything undecided after 2
  sessions is rejected or escalated. It is not left open.
- **Build** starts once an owner is named at the point of acceptance. No
  owner means no acceptance.
- **Review** is the merge gate. It needs an accessibility check, a
  provenance tag, a decision record entry, and a deviation record if the
  component departs from a GOV.UK pattern. All 4 are required.
- **Publish** means released, versioned, announced and added to the site.
  Nothing lands silently.
- **Maintain** happens quarterly. The group reviews upstream changes,
  deprecations and drift. A component with no owner is a candidate for
  deprecation.

You can see where a submission has got to from its status: raised,
accepted, in build, in review, in kit, or deprecated.

Rejected submissions are recorded with the reason.

## The 4 fields in a deviation record

1. The GOV.UK pattern you departed from.
2. What the product needed that the pattern didn't provide.
3. What you built instead.
4. What would need to be true to promote it to a general pattern.

The fourth field makes this a forward-looking record rather than a log of
apologies. Accumulated deviations are the evidence base for defining
emerging AI design patterns later. Without a consistent shape, that work
starts from opinion.

## If another department built it first

Copy their styles and JavaScript into the i.AI kit. Don't add their design
system as a dependency.
