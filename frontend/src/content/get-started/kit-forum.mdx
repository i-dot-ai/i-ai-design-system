---
title: "Contribute to the Design kit"
description: How to contribute a component, pattern or deviation to the i.AI kit.
---

Anyone in i.AI can contribute.

## 1. Look first

Check the kit site. If it exists, use it. If something nearly works, say
so in step 2 — a near-miss is a contribution in itself, because it tells
where the kit falls short and can be picked up.

## 2. Sense-check in Slack

Post in the channel before you open a PR: what you need, what you tried,
what nearly works, and a screenshot or link. Informal and fast, it is not
approval and it does not block you — build locally if you want to keep
moving. What it does is catch duplication early and let someone point you
at prior art before you spend a day on it.

## 3. Raise a PR against the kit repo

Build it in the open. The PR template tells you exactly what a kit site
page needs to contain to be ready:

- what it is, in a sentence
- when to use it
- when not to use it
- the HTML, copyable, with each variant shown
- options and modifiers
- accessibility notes, including anything the component does not enforce
- provenance tag and status
- linked decision records and any deviation record

## 4. Three approvals, asynchronously

Every PR needs sign-off in three areas before it merges:

- **Code maintainability** — the technical maintainer
- **Accessibility** — the accessibility reviewer
- **Design quality** — a design reviewer

## 5. Merge, publish, announce

Released, versioned, site updated, posted in the channel. You consume it
in your product immediately.
