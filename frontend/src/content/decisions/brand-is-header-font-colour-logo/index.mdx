## Context

This follows how GOV.UK itself separates brand from the rest of an interface.
Keeping brand to these few things means we can use GOV.UK Frontend across all
our products and switch a product's brand easily.

## Decision

Brand is the header, the font, the brand colour and the logo. Nothing else
counts as brand.
