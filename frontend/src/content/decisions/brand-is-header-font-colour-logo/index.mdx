## Context

[Decision 002](/decisions-record/branding-determined-by-audience) states that 
we will need products that are branded either full GOV.UK or as a government 
department.

We therefore need to know how to switch between the different brands in a way 
that enables us to use the same functionality to avoid having two design 
systems.

The [Service Manual guidance on when your product is not in 
GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/making-your-service-look-like-govuk) 
states that to use the GOV.UK Frontend when you are not GOV.UK your 
product must **not**:

- identify itself as being part of GOV.UK
- use the crown or GOV.UK logotype in the header
- use the GDS Transport typeface
- suggest that it’s an official UK government website if it’s not
- use the GOV.UK brand colours

## Decision

Brand is the prodcut name, header, font, brand colour and the logo. Changing 
these is enough to shift the two brands set out in [decision 
002](/decisions-record/branding-determined-by-audience)

## Consequences

This also allows us to use the GOV.UK Frontend for all our products.
