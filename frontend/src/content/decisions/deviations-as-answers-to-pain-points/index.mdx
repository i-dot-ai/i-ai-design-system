## Context

GOV.UK Frontend has been designed with ease of use as a grounding principle. 
Therefore, we only want to deviate from this where there is a real benefit to
the usability of our apps.

There often can be real benefits to the friction caused by trying to make a complex 
product work with GOV.UK Frontend in that simplifying the overall experience to 
work within GOV.UK can often bring the best benefits to the user.

However, as stated in [decision 005](/decisions-record/adapt-govuk-frontend-to-our-needs)
we have a slightly different user base and will need to deviate at times. We 
also don't want to repeat design work where it has already been done in i.AI.

## Decision

We will record departures as answers to pain points rather than as starting
places for products. For example, a product should not start with a two panel
layout, but can use one if a linear flow is making the app less usable.
