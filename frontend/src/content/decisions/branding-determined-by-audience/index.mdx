## Context

A product's brand should follow who it is for, so that the branding reflects
the users and the standards that apply to them rather than the team that built
it.

## Decision

Products are branded as one of two options, depending on their audience:

- **A government department**, when the product is for a specific department's
  use. If it is not tied to a specific department, the department is i.AI and
  the generic header is used.
- **GOV.UK**, when the product is for use by the general public or across the
  public sector.

The department brand is the default. A product that is experimental or unsure
starts here and can switch to GOV.UK later.

## Consequence

Most products start as department-branded and move to GOV.UK as they are rolled
out more widely, rather than starting as GOV.UK.
