## Context

The design kit needs to be kept up to date without relying on a dedicated team,
which we do not have. A light, regular process lets it be maintained alongside
people's normal work.

## Decision

We will create a slack channel for advice and general contribution actions. We 
will set up a PR system and contribution guides to help people get as far along 
the contribution workflow without the need of help.
