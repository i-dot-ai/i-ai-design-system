## Context

The GOV.UK Design System carries years of work on usability and accessibility,
and gives products a familiar government feel that people trust. Starting from
it means we do not repeat design work that has already been done, and it makes
handing a product to another government department easier.

## Decision

We will build from the GOV.UK Frontend first.
