## Context

GOV.UK Frontend carries years of work on usability and accessibility, and 
gives products a familiar government feel that people trust. Starting from
it means we do not repeat design work that has already been done, and it makes
handing a product to another government department easier.

It also means we avoid significant maintentance work involved in starting a 
design system from scratch.

It was felt that third party component libraries often have accessibility 
gaps which would require an audit and regular checks.

## Decision

We will use GOV.UK Frontend as our basis.

## Consequences

We will need a system to keep track of changes that happen to GOV.UK Frontend.
