## Context

We need one place that holds our frontend and design decisions so they are
shared across i.AI. A single public website, written in plain markup, works as
one source of truth for both people and agents, and is easy to keep up to date.

## Decision

We will publish all our guidance and the reasoning behind it on the design kit
website.
