## Context

Our starting point is GOV.UK, and we only move away from it when we need to.
Writing down each departure, and the reason for it, keeps a clear record of why
a product differs from GOV.UK and helps others facing the same problem.

## Decision

We will document where we have departed from the GOV.UK Design System and why.
