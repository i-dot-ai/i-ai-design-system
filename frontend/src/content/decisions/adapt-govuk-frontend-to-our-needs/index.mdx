## Context

The GOV.UK Design System was made for informational websites and forms used
occasionally by the general public. Our products are mostly used repeatedly by
the same people to carry out more complex tasks. Every i.AI product that has
used the GOV.UK Design System has needed to adapt it in some way to fit this.

## Decision

We will adapt and extend the GOV.UK Frontend to meet the needs of our products.
