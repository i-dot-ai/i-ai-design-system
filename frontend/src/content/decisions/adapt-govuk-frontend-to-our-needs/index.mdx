## Context

GOV.UK Frontend was made for informational websites and critical forms used 
by the general public often used only once or very rarely.

Our products are more accurately described as "apps" which allow civil servants 
to repeatedly complete complex tasks. 

We are designing for a different user profile than GOV.UK Frontend was originally 
designed for. This means that deviations are likely to be needed to meet the 
needs of our users.

Where are products have tried to follow GOV.UK Frontend in the past, departures 
and deviations have always been needed to some extent.

## Decision

We will adapt and extend the GOV.UK Frontend to meet the needs of our products.

## Consequences

We will need to decide our approach to deviating from GOV.UK Frontend. See [decision 
006](/decisions-record/document-departures-from-govuk) and [decision 
007](/decisions-record/deviations-as-answers-to-pain-points)
