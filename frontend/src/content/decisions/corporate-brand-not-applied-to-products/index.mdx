## Context

The i.AI brand is our corporate brand, used for things like our website,
decks, communications and recruitment. Applying it to the products we build
would blur the line between how we present i.AI as an organisation and the
products themselves, which should be led by the needs of their users and by
government standards.

## Decision

We will not use the i.AI brand to brand the products we build.
