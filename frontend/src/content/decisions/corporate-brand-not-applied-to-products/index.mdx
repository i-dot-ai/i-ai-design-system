## Context

The i.AI brand is our corporate brand, used for things like our website,
decks, communications and recruitment. It was designed primarily to 
advertise i.AI to potential hires and present us as a modern place to work.

We have different aims for our products. Namely, to be easy to use, 
accessible and trustworthy.

## Decision

We will not use the i.AI brand to brand the products we build.
